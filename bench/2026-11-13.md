# Bench notes, 2026-11-13 (Gateway 12, web app "© Nokia 2021-2024/2025")

What the bench logs of 13 November showed, and what the station does about it.
No serials, IMEIs or passwords here.

## خلاصه (فارسی)

- اسم Wi-Fi متفاوت (اسمی که مشتری گذاشته) دیگر قبل از Reset، FAIL نمی‌شود و ۲ دقیقه هم منتظر نمی‌ماند.
  بعد از Reset و Ping دوباره وارد روتر می‌شود و اسم را می‌خواند. اگر اسم برچسب برگشته بود PASS است،
  و همین ثابت می‌کند که تنظیمات مشتری پاک شده. اگر هنوز اسم مشتری بود، TESTING و DATA-WIPE هر دو FAIL می‌شوند.
- Ping بعد از Reset با آدرس ثابت روی کامپیوتر (docs/linux-pc-network.md) حدود 1m40s است.
  این زمان روشن شدن خود روتر است.
- اولین کلیک روی Network map بعد از ورود همیشه حدود ۲۰ ثانیه منتظر می‌ماند،
  چون روتر نقشه را دیر می‌سازد. فعلاً از سمت ما راهی برای کوتاه کردنش پیدا نشده.

## Wi-Fi name

- Used units keep the customer's Wi-Fi name. Before: the station waited
  `connect_s` (2 min) for the name to change, then failed the unit.
- One unit failed on its Wi-Fi name, was reset, and passed it on its next run:
  so the factory reset puts the label's name back.
- Now: a name that is not the label's is INFO before the reset (no wait). After
  the reset and the ping the station signs in again and reads it once more
  ("Wi-Fi name after reset"). The label's name back = PASS, and it is also the
  proof that the wipe happened. Still the customer's name = FAIL for
  ROUTER-TESTING and ROUTER-DATA-WIPE. A run with no reset fails it as before.
- First real run with this (22:54): INFO before, PASS after the reset (21 s for
  the second login), unit PASS.

## A ping that answers too soon after the reset

On four units that carried a customer's Wi-Fi name, 8.8.8.8 answered 1 to 31 s
after "router went down". A unit that really restarts takes ~1m40s. "Went
down" only means the web page stopped answering, so on those units the reset
may not have restarted the router at that point. Their DATA-WIPE PASS is not
trusted: test them again. The Wi-Fi name check after the reset now catches it.

## Ping time

- With the fixed address on the PC's cable, the ping takes 1m38s to 1m43s on
  every normal unit (it was up to ~4 min with DHCP).
- One unit with a customer's settings took 3m33s (22:55). The PC already had
  the fixed address, so this time is the router's own (probably two restarts).

## The ~20 s before the Network map click

- Every unit: the first click on the map after login goes ~20 s after the
  Overview loaded. Before login (prelogin page) the map is there after 1.3 s.
- The station now logs why a click waits more than `map_click_s` (3 s). The
  22:54 run said "waiting for locator div.mapClickArea": the map element is
  not in the page at all for ~20 s. So nothing on our side can click it
  sooner; the click from inside the page does not help here either.

## 5G

Every unit with a good SIM: "Connected" in 2 s. One unit on software 1.2404 had
modem SIM state "None": SIM FAIL, 5G failed at once without waiting (correct).
One unit on software 1.2402 took 100 s to load its page and showed IMEI "N/A":
an old build to look at separately.

## Scanner

Some scans came in broken: `[` in place of `{`, `'` in place of `"`, a
missing comma. These are scanner typing errors; a second scan reads fine.
